### D7 — New methodology document: responsibility-sharing.ja.md, applies beyond collaboration-planning
**Date**: 2026-09-05
**What**: Added `methodology/responsibility-sharing.ja.md` at hfu's explicit request, prompted by a principle he stated directly: generative-AI output must be checked by whoever generated it, and this applies to Staff (whose output — Map Intent, map links, collaboration plans, narratives — is itself AI-generated) just as much as to any other generative-AI use. Since Cartographer is Staff's faceless puppet with no judgment of its own, responsibility for what gets rendered traces back through Staff to the User who invoked it — meaning responsibility is *shared* with the User, not solely borne by Staff/Cartographer/Library. hfu was explicit this is not a liability-dodging maneuver but an honest model of where responsibility actually sits, and that communicating it clearly to Users is important for `staccato-ecosystem` earning trust. Unlike the other nine `methodology/*.ja.md` documents (all scoped to the collaboration-planning capability layered on top of map-generation, per `request-types.ja.md`'s type B), this one applies to Staff's output in general, including plain map requests (type A) — noted explicitly in the doc and in `methodology/README.md`'s index. Cross-referenced from `quality-checklist.ja.md` (a new checklist item) and connected explicitly to the "展開する立場" (intermediary) audience concept from D5/`process.ja.md`, and to `staccato-spec`'s ADR 0001 (Faceless Cartographer's human-mediated handoff) as the existing architectural mechanism this document's guidance builds on top of.
**Why**: this is a genuinely different kind of principle from the other nine — not a step in planning a collaboration, but a cross-cutting honesty/trust obligation for any Staff output. It also retroactively explains part of why the (still-unreviewed) volcano Staff draft's choice to serve "local intermediaries" rather than the general public matters: an intermediary audience is exactly where this shared-responsibility chain is most consequential, since their own audience (residents, students, guests) never sees Staff's output directly — only what the intermediary chose to pass on after checking it.

### D2 — Content-document language policy: `.ja.md`/`.en.md` pairs, alternating primary by context
**Date**: 2026-09-03
**What**: Confirmed with hfu that this repo's *content* documents (the value-proposition/methodology deliverables — distinct from meta-docs like this file, `README.md`, `HANDOVER.md`, and boilerplate like `LICENSE`, which stay English-only per the existing convention) are managed as `.ja.md`/`.en.md` pairs per topic rather than single bilingual files. A bare `.md` means not yet split. Work alternates by context rather than staying continuously bilingual: in a domestic-implementation (国内実施) context, `.ja.md` is primary and content accumulates there; when switching to an international-cooperation (国際協力) context, `.ja.md` → `.en.md` is synced (translated) first, then `.en.md` becomes primary for that stretch of work. Sync happens at context transitions, not on every edit.
**Why**: hfu's explicit framing — this repo's founding concept includes organic linkage between domestic implementation and international cooperation as parallel, alternating modes of work, not simultaneous bilingual authoring. Keeping both languages continuously in sync on every edit would slow down whichever mode is currently active; syncing only at the transition matches how the work actually happens. Repo-local experiment, not intended to propagate to other `dwg7`/`UNopenGIS` repos.
