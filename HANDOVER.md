The durable, actionable substance: Staff's job, for a given place, is to recognize which *one* relevant concern/significance a specific asker actually has and surface only that to Cartographer, rather than presenting an undifferentiated overview of everything a place could mean to different people (Library holds the full range of what's available; Cartographer renders whatever Staff selected, without judgment of its own). Strategy going forward is explicitly **depth-first, not breadth-first** — work through one existing, real connection at a time rather than parallelizing across many domains. Named candidates, in the order raised: FERSPAS (done), Vientiane (`dwg7/vientiane-planning-map`, JICA stadium-district planning), **volcano (in progress, see below)**, PLATEAU (`dwg7/plateau-mago-implicit`, Muroran/Sarabetsu — Muroran is a port/harbor case), education (methodology already covers this). - **New methodology document, `responsibility-sharing.ja.md` (D7)**: at hfu's explicit request — Staff's output (Map Intent, map links, collaboration plans, narratives) is itself AI-generated content, and the principle "whoever generates it is responsible for checking it" applies to Staff's User just as it would to any generative-AI use; Cartographer, as Staff's faceless puppet, adds no independent judgment, so responsibility traces back through Staff to the User. This isn't a liability-dodge — it's an honest model of where responsibility sits, and communicating it clearly to Users is explicitly framed as important for this repo's trust-building. Unlike the other methodology docs, this one applies to Staff's output in general (including plain map requests), not just the collaboration-planning layer.
