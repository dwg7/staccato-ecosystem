- **New methodology document, `responsibility-sharing.ja.md` (D7)**: at hfu's explicit request — Staff's output (Map Intent, map links, collaboration plans, narratives) is itself AI-generated content, and the principle "whoever generates it is responsible for checking it" applies to Staff's User just as it would to any generative-AI use; Cartographer, as Staff's faceless puppet, adds no independent judgment, so responsibility traces back through Staff to the User. This isn't a liability-dodge — it's an honest model of where responsibility sits, and communicating it clearly to Users is explicitly framed as important for this repo's trust-building. A second framing was added the same day at hfu's request: growing societal skepticism toward generative AI ("it can be wrong") is this project's ally, not a headwind — staccato's output (a real map link, a dated dossier) is cheap to verify compared to freeform AI prose, so honesty about fallibility is a differentiator, not just a disclosure. Unlike the other methodology docs, this one applies to Staff's output in general (including plain map requests), not just the collaboration-planning layer.

Three diagnoses from this session were wrong, and `dwg7/spiccato` needed no fix. **The reusable operational fact**: `volca`'s pane, `dwg7/spiccato`'s session's pane, and this session's pane all run `document.hidden` with **zero `requestAnimationFrame` frames** — measured, not assumed — and nothing paints in that state no matter what you do in-page. **Before diagnosing any rendering problem from a browser pane, check `document.hidden` and whether rAF fires.** Screenshots still come back from a dead pane, which is what made this look like a real observation.
